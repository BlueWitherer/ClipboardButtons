# Easy Clipboard
**Add clipboard UI to every text input field.**

*by [Cheeseworks](user:6408873)!*

> ![✳️](frame:collaborationIcon_001.png) <cg>*This mod has settings you can utilize to customize your experience.*</c>

---

## About
This mod adds a copy and a paste button on every text input field you can find in the game when you focus on them!

---

### Customization
In <cg>this mod's settings</c>, you can find the options you need to <cy>re-scale the buttons</c> to your liking as well as <cy>adjust their opacity</c>, among others. Feel free to tweak these as you need!
